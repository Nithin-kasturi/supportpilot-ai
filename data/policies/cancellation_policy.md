# SwiftParcel Cancellation Policy

## Cancellation before first scan
A SwiftParcel order cancelled before the parcel's first scan at pickup or drop-off receives a full shipping fee refund with no cancellation fee.

## Scheduled pickups
A SwiftParcel scheduled pickup can be cancelled without a fee at least 2 hours before the pickup window starts. Cancelling less than 2 hours before the window starts incurs a $3.00 late-cancellation fee, deducted from the refund.

## Cancellation after first scan
A SwiftParcel order can still be cancelled after the first scan but before the parcel is out for delivery. The parcel is returned to the sender, and the refund is the shipping fee minus a $5.00 return handling fee.

## Out for delivery or delivered
A SwiftParcel order cannot be cancelled once the parcel is out for delivery or delivered. A recipient refusing delivery does not count as a cancellation and is not refundable.

## How to cancel
A SwiftParcel customer cancels through customer support by giving the order ID. The parcel's scan status at the time SwiftParcel receives the request decides which cancellation rule applies.

## Cancellation refunds
SwiftParcel cancellation refunds follow the Refund Policy: amounts under $50.00 can be approved automatically, amounts of $50.00 or more are reviewed within 3 business days, and approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
