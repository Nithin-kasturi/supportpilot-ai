# SwiftParcel Shipping Policy

## Delivery services and estimated times
SwiftParcel ships domestically only. Estimated delivery times count from the dispatch date:
- Express: 1 to 2 business days.
- Standard: 3 to 5 business days.
- Economy: 6 to 10 business days.

## Business days and cutoff time
SwiftParcel business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. A parcel scanned at the origin depot before 2:00 PM local time is dispatched the same business day. A parcel scanned after 2:00 PM is dispatched the next business day.

## Parcel limits and prohibited items
SwiftParcel accepts parcels up to 30 kg with a longest side of 120 cm. Prohibited items are weapons, flammable liquids, illegal goods, cash, live animals and perishable food. Parcels over the limits or containing prohibited items are not accepted.

## Tracking
SwiftParcel issues a tracking number when the order is placed. Tracking updates at each scan and can lag by up to 24 hours.

## Delivery attempts and holding
SwiftParcel makes up to 3 delivery attempts on consecutive business days. After the third failed attempt, the parcel is held at the nearest depot for 7 calendar days. Parcels not claimed after that period are returned to the sender.

## Delayed parcels
A SwiftParcel parcel is delayed when it has not arrived 2 business days after the estimated delivery date. A delayed Express parcel qualifies for a shipping fee refund under the Refund Policy. Delayed Standard and Economy parcels do not receive automatic compensation, but customers can contact support to investigate. Delays caused by severe weather or natural disasters do not qualify for the Express shipping fee refund.

## Lost parcels
A SwiftParcel parcel can be reported as potentially lost when tracking shows no update for 7 consecutive days. SwiftParcel investigates for up to 5 business days. A confirmed lost parcel is refunded under the Refund Policy: the shipping fee plus the declared item value, capped at $500.00.
