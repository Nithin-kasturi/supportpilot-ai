# SwiftParcel Escalation Policy

## Support hours
SwiftParcel customer support is open Monday to Friday 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM Eastern Time and Saturday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Eastern Time. Support is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Support levels
SwiftParcel handles complaints in three levels: Level 1 is front-line support, Level 2 is senior support, and Level 3 is the customer resolution manager.

## Escalation to Level 2
A SwiftParcel complaint moves to Level 2 when:
- The customer requests escalation after receiving a first response.
- The issue is unresolved after 2 business days.
- The customer contacts SwiftParcel for the third time about the same issue.
- A refund review is still pending after the promised 3 business days.
Level 2 responds within 1 business day.

## Escalation to Level 3
A SwiftParcel complaint moves to Level 3 when the customer disputes a Level 2 decision or reports staff misconduct. Legal threats, safety concerns, suspected fraud and data-privacy complaints go directly to Level 3 and are acknowledged within 4 business hours. Level 3 gives a final written decision within 2 business days.

## Information required
A SwiftParcel escalation needs the order ID, any earlier ticket reference numbers, a short description of the issue, and supporting evidence such as photographs for damage complaints. An escalation without an order ID cannot be processed.

## Decision authority
Escalation decisions are made by SwiftParcel staff, not by the virtual assistant. The virtual assistant can explain the escalation process but cannot promise an outcome.
