# SwiftParcel Refund Policy

## Scope
This fictional policy covers refunds for shipping orders placed with SwiftParcel. "Order value" means the shipping fee paid plus the declared item value on the order. A SwiftParcel refund can cover the shipping fee and, for lost or damaged parcels, the declared item value.

## Eligible refund reasons
A SwiftParcel customer can qualify for a refund only in these cases:
1. The order is cancelled before delivery, under the SwiftParcel Cancellation Policy.
2. An Express parcel is delivered late, under the SwiftParcel Shipping Policy (shipping fee only).
3. The parcel is confirmed lost.
4. A damage claim is approved under the SwiftParcel Damaged Parcel Policy.
5. SwiftParcel made a billing error, such as a duplicate charge.

## Non-refundable situations
SwiftParcel does not refund:
- Failed deliveries caused by an incorrect or incomplete address supplied by the customer.
- Deliveries refused by the recipient.
- Parcels containing prohibited items.
- Parcels left unclaimed after the 7-day depot holding period.
- Late Economy or Standard deliveries (support can investigate, but no automatic refund applies).
- Change of mind after the parcel has been delivered.

## Refund request time limit
A SwiftParcel refund request must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the delivery date. For lost or undelivered parcels, the 30 days count from the original estimated delivery date. Damage reports have a stricter 48-hour limit, described in the Damaged Parcel Policy. Requests after the limit are not eligible, except where a documented SwiftParcel system fault prevented the customer from submitting the request.

## Approval thresholds
- A refund under $50.00 that meets all eligibility conditions can be approved automatically.
- A refund of $50.00 or more requires approval by the SwiftParcel support team, who give a decision within 3 business days.
- The declared item value is capped at $500.00 per parcel. A refund never exceeds the shipping fee plus the declared value.

## Refund payment
Approved SwiftParcel refunds are paid to the original payment method only, within 5 to 7 business days after approval. SwiftParcel does not pay refunds in cash or to a different payment method.

## Role of the virtual assistant
The SwiftParcel virtual assistant can explain refund rules but cannot approve or process refunds. Eligibility decisions are made by SwiftParcel systems and staff.
