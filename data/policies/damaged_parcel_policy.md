# SwiftParcel Damaged Parcel Policy

## Reporting deadline
A damaged SwiftParcel parcel must be reported within 48 hours of the delivery time shown in tracking. Reports made after 48 hours are not eligible for a damage claim, except where a documented SwiftParcel system fault prevented the customer from reporting.

## Required evidence
A SwiftParcel damage claim requires:
- At least 2 clear photographs: one of the damaged item and one of the outer packaging showing the shipping label.
- The order ID.
The customer must keep the item and packaging until the claim is decided. Photographs may be added up to 3 business days after the report is filed, but a claim cannot be approved until the evidence is received.

## Approval by order value
- A damage claim with complete evidence and an order value under $50.00 can be approved automatically.
- A damage claim with an order value of $50.00 or more requires approval by the SwiftParcel support team, who give a decision within 3 business days.
- A claim with incomplete evidence is never approved automatically, regardless of order value.
Order value means the shipping fee plus the declared item value.

## Exclusions
SwiftParcel does not accept damage claims for:
- Damage caused by inadequate packaging by the sender, such as a fragile item shipped without padding.
- Items that were already damaged before shipping.
- Prohibited items.

## Outcome of an approved claim
An approved SwiftParcel damage claim is refunded under the Refund Policy: to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days, capped at $500.00 declared value plus the shipping fee.
